================================================================================
SAGACIOUS WASHING CENTER — FINANCIAL EQUATIONS & CALCULATION REFERENCE
================================================================================

This document provides a clean, simple, plain-text reference for every formula
used in the SAGA Washing Center ERP system.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ORDER CALCULATIONS (orders.js)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. ITEMS SUBTOTAL
   Total price of all clothes/items in the order before any discounts.

   Items Subtotal = Sum of (Quantity x Unit Price for each line item)


B. DISCOUNT AMOUNT
   Discount applied only to laundry items (not applied to delivery or extra fees).

   Discount Amount = Items Subtotal x (Discount Rate % / 100)


C. ORDER GRAND TOTAL
   Order Grand Total = (Items Subtotal - Discount Amount) + Delivery Charge + Extra Payment


D. ORDER STATUS
   - Paid:   If Advance Payment >= Order Grand Total
   - Unpaid: If Advance Payment < Order Grand Total



--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INVOICE CALCULATIONS (invoice.js & financials.js)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. GROSS INVOICE TOTAL
   Total bill before any customer dispute/damage deductions.

   Gross Invoice Total = (Items Subtotal - Discount Amount) + Delivery Charge + Extra Payment


B. FINAL NET PAYABLE TOTAL
   Final amount owed by customer after subtracting approved deductions
   (e.g., damaged linen, customer concessions).

   Final Net Payable = Gross Invoice Total - Deductions Amount


C. TOTAL PAID
   All money received so far from the customer for this invoice.

   Total Paid = Advance Payment + Sum of all Recorded Payments


D. BALANCE DUE (OUTSTANDING AMOUNT)
   Balance Due = Final Net Payable - Total Paid  (Minimum is 0)


E. INVOICE PAYMENT STATUS
   - Paid:           If Balance Due <= 0
   - Partially Paid: If Total Paid > 0 AND Balance Due > 0
   - Unpaid:         If Total Paid == 0



--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EXPENSE & TRANSPORT CALCULATIONS (expenses.js, transport.js)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. MULTI-MONTH EXPENSE AVERAGING (AMORTIZATION)
   Spreads long-term expenses (e.g. 12-month insurance, annual license) evenly
   across all covered months.

   Monthly Expense Share = Total Expense Amount / Months Covered
   (Example: LKR 120,000 for 12 months = LKR 10,000 per month)


B. CHEMICAL STOCK COSTS (TWO COSTING OPTIONS)
   Option 1: Cash Outflow (Purchases)
   Sum of all chemical purchase invoices in the period.
   Chemical Expense = Sum of all Chemical Stock Purchase Amounts (type = 'IN')

   Option 2: COGS (Actual Inventory Consumed)
   Value of chemicals actually used during washing operations.
   Chemical Expense (COGS) = Sum of (Quantity Used x Purchase Unit Price) (type = 'OUT')


C. TRANSPORT FUEL COST PER TRIP
   Cost Per KM = Fuel Price per Litre / KM per Litre
   Trip Fuel Cost = Trip Distance (in KM) x Cost Per KM



--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EXECUTIVE ANALYTICS & PROFIT / LOSS (analytics.js)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. BOOKED REVENUE VS. NET BOOKED REVENUE (ACCRUAL BASIS)
   Gross Revenue = Sum of all Order Grand Totals created in the period
   Net Booked Revenue = Gross Revenue - Sum of all Invoice Deductions in the period


B. CASH COLLECTED (REALIZED CASH FLOW BASIS)
   Actual cash received in hand during the selected period.
   Cash Collected = Sum of Advance Payments + Sum of all Payments Received in the period


C. TOTAL OPERATING EXPENSES (OPEX)
   Total Expenses = Amortized General Expenses + Chemical Expenses + Transport Fuel Costs


D. NET OPERATING PROFIT
   Net Operating Profit = Net Booked Revenue - Total Expenses


E. PROFIT MARGIN %
   Profit Margin % = (Net Operating Profit / Net Booked Revenue) x 100


F. OPERATING COST RATIO %
   Cost Ratio % = (Total Expenses / Net Booked Revenue) x 100


G. AVERAGE ORDER VALUE (AOV)
   Average Order Value = Net Booked Revenue / Total Number of Orders


H. UNCOLLECTED RECEIVABLES
   Total unpaid money owed to the business across all customers.
   Total Uncollected Receivables = Sum of Balance Due across all Unpaid Invoices
